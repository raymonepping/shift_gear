      What this proves, and what it does not

      Proves

      • Vault KV v2 versions really change (the rotation add-on writes them).
      • Two workloads with separate identities each receive the new version from their own Vault Agent.
      • A separate verifier accepts the new key and refuses the old one: at once in strict mode, after the grace period otherwise.
      • The client reloads without restarting; the gap between the two parties is measured, not assumed.

      Does not prove

      • Issuer-side rotation by an external provider: here the application owns the credential, by design.
      • Anything about a real door: the protected resource is fictional, the cryptographic check is not.
      • Vault authorisation from the connectivity row: it shows reachability only.
